Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 17, 2026
1. Overview
This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our Roku screensaver application ("Application"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency.
2. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal data.
No Analytics: The Application does not use third-party tracking, performance measurement tools, or analytics software.
No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide any credentials to use the Application.
No Device Identifiers: The Application does not capture, store, or transmit your Roku IP address, device ID, location, or user activity logs.
3. App Functionality
The Application operates entirely locally on your Roku device. Its only function is to display the time and respond to user keypresses to close the screensaver. No internet data transmission occurs as a result of using this Application.
4. Children’s Privacy
Because the Application collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13, and fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
5. Third-Party Services
The Application does not contain third-party advertisements, external links, or integrations with third-party web services.
